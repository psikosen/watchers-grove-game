How to Play

The Watchers' Grove is a memory game where you must repeat patterns shown by the ancient eyes.

  • Watch the eyes light up in sequence
  • Click them in the same order
  • Each level adds a new eye to the pattern
  • Manage your Sanity, Insight, and Power
  • Use power-ups strategically to survive longer

Game Modes:

  • Story: Progress through levels with narrative
  • Survival: How long can you last?
  • Zen: Relaxed mode with no time pressure
  • Boss: Face powerful guardians
