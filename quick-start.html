The Watcher's Grove 👁️

🎮 Quick Start

  1. Watch the eyes blink in a sequence
  2. Remember the pattern they create
  3. Click the eyes in the exact same order
  4. Progress through increasingly complex patterns
  5. Survive with your sanity intact!

🎮 Controls

Click Select eyes in the pattern sequence ESC Pause the game Ctrl+S Save your progress 🔊 Toggle sound (top-left corner)

👁️ Eye Types

  • Brown Eyes - Standard eyes
  • Blue Eyes - Ancient eyes grant bonus insight
  • Red Eyes - Corrupted eyes damage sanity
  • Golden Eyes - Rare eyes give bonus points
💡 Pro Tip: Listen to the musical notes! Each eye position plays a unique tone, creating a melody you can memorize alongside the visual pattern.

🏆 Quick Goals

  • Reach Level 5 to unlock "Survivor" achievement
  • Complete a level without mistakes for "Perfect Vision"
  • Build a 10x combo for massive score multipliers
  • Try all 6 seasonal themes (bottom-left in menu)
  • Create your first custom level in the editor
🌟 Secret: The grove remembers dedicated players. Unlock special content by collecting achievements and clicking many eyes!
