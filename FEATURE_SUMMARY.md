# The Watcher's Grove - Feature Summary

## Complete Feature List (Ultimate Edition v3.0)

### 🎮 Core Gameplay
- ✅ Pattern-matching mechanics with eye sequences
- ✅ Progressive difficulty system
- ✅ Sanity/Insight/Power stat system
- ✅ Musical note system for each eye
- ✅ Combo and streak tracking
- ✅ Score multipliers based on performance

### 🎯 Game Modes (6 Total)
1. **Story Mode** - 6 levels with narrative progression
2. **Survival Mode** - Endless patterns with power drain
3. **Zen Mode** - Relaxing gameplay with no penalties
4. **Boss Rush Mode** - Face 4 unique bosses with special mechanics
5. **Practice Mode** - Customizable training environment
6. **Level Editor** - Create and share custom patterns

### 👹 Boss System
- **The Watcher** - Introductory boss with basic patterns
- **Ancient Seer** - Faster patterns with more complexity
- **Eye of Madness** - Chaotic patterns that test sanity
- **The All-Seeing** - Ultimate challenge with all mechanics

Boss Features:
- Health bars and unique attack patterns
- Bosses heal when player makes mistakes
- Special boss eye mechanics
- Epic defeat animations
- Boss-specific power-ups

### 💊 Power-Up System (6 Types)
1. **Slow Time** ⏱️ - Reduces pattern playback speed
2. **Reveal One** 👁️ - Shows the next eye in sequence
3. **Skip Level** ⏭️ - Bypass difficult patterns
4. **Sanity Boost** 💊 - Restore mental health
5. **Shield** 🛡️ - Absorb one mistake (Boss mode)
6. **Multi-Click** ✨ - Click affects nearby eyes (Boss mode)
