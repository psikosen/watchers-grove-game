# The Watcher's Grove - Enhanced Edition

A deeply atmospheric horror-puzzle game where you must commune with an ancient tree covered in watching eyes. Now featuring multiple game modes, power-ups, achievements, and enhanced visuals!

## 🎮 Game Modes

### Story Mode
Experience the full narrative as you uncover the tree's ancient secrets through 6 increasingly challenging levels. Each level reveals more of the dark mystery.

### Survival Mode
How long can you last? Your power drains with each move, patterns get harder, and the eyes grow hungrier. Compete for the highest score!

### Zen Mode
A relaxing experience with gentle patterns, minimal penalties, and soothing musical sequences. Perfect for practice or meditation.

## 🎯 How to Play

1. **Launch**: Run `./play.sh` or open `index.html` in your browser
2. **Choose Your Path**: Select a game mode and difficulty level
3. **Watch Carefully**: The eyes will blink in a specific sequence
4. **Repeat the Pattern**: Click the eyes in the exact order they blinked
5. **Use Power-Ups**: Strategic abilities to help you survive
6. **Unlock Achievements**: Discover all 8 hidden achievements
7. **Master the Grove**: Complete all modes and claim your place on the leaderboard

## 🌟 New Features

### Power-Up System
- **⏱️ Slow Time**: Slows down pattern playback (costs 20 power)
- **👁️ Reveal One**: Shows the next eye in the sequence (3 uses)
- **⏭️ Skip Level**: Bypass a difficult pattern (1 use)
- **💊 Sanity Boost**: Restore 30% sanity (2 uses)

### Enhanced Visuals
- **Living Background**: The tree breathes and moves with organic animations
- **Particle Effects**: Enhanced visual feedback with special effects
- **Eye Types**: Regular, Ancient (blue), and Corrupted (red) eyes
- **Atmospheric Fog**: Dynamic fog effects that respond to your sanity
- **Screen Shake**: Visceral feedback when you make mistakes

### Achievement System
- First Contact - Click your first eye
- Perfect Vision - Complete a level without mistakes
- Survivor - Reach level 5
- Embrace Madness - Reach 0 sanity
- Speed Demon - Complete a sequence in under 3 seconds
- Eye Collector - Click 100 eyes total
- Master of the Grove - Complete Story Mode
- Ancient Knowledge - Reach 100% insight

### Stats & Progression
- **Sanity**: Your mental health (lose on mistakes)
- **Insight**: Knowledge gained (increases with success)
- **Power**: Energy for abilities (drains in Survival mode)
- **Score System**: Points based on speed, combos, and difficulty
- **Combo Multiplier**: Chain correct patterns for bonus points

### Difficulty Levels
- **Novice Seeker**: More sanity, slower patterns, power bonuses
- **Adept Scholar**: Balanced gameplay (default)
- **Master Occultist**: Less sanity, faster patterns, higher scores
- **Elder One**: Minimal sanity, lightning patterns, maximum challenge

### Audio Enhancements
- **Musical Patterns**: Each eye plays a note from a musical scale
- **Ambient Drone**: Atmospheric background soundscape
- **Dynamic Sound**: Audio responds to your actions and game state
- **3D Positional Audio**: Sounds emanate from eye positions

## 🎮 Controls

- **Mouse Click**: Select eyes
- **ESC**: Pause game
- **Power-Up Buttons**: Click to activate abilities

## 💡 Pro Tips

- **Pattern Recognition**: Focus on groups of 3-4 eyes at a time
- **Audio Cues**: Each position has a unique tone - use your ears!
- **Combo Strategy**: Perfect sequences multiply your score
- **Power Management**: Save power-ups for later levels
- **Difficulty Scaling**: Start on Novice to learn patterns
- **Ancient Eyes**: Blue eyes give bonus insight when clicked correctly
- **Corrupted Eyes**: Red eyes appear in Survival mode - avoid them!

## 🏆 Scoring System

- Base points: 100 × current level
- Combo multiplier: ×(combo + 1)
- Difficulty bonus: ×1.5 (Master), ×2 (Elder)
- Achievement bonus: +500 per unlock
- Speed bonus: Complete patterns quickly for extra points
